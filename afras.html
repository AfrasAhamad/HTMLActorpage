Sri Lanka is a beautiful island nation located in the Indian Ocean, known for its rich culture, ancient history, and stunning natural landscapes. The country is famous for its golden beaches, lush tea plantations, and diverse wildlife. Sri Lanka has a multicultural society with Sinhalese, Tamil, Muslim, and other communities living together. Agriculture, tourism, and exports such as tea and rubber play important roles in the economy. With its warm climate, scenic mountains, and historical sites like Sigiriya and Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka attracts visitors from around the world.
